      Context for investors

      A world-scale resource

      Argentina holds the world's second-largest technically recoverable shale gas resources and the fourth-largest shale oil resources, most of them in Vaca Muerta.

      US International Trade Administration

      Incentives for large projects

      The RIGI regime gives large investments tax, customs and FX stability for 30 years. By August 2026 the government listed 22 approved projects worth USD 47 billion, six of them in oil and gas.

      Official RIGI portal, Ministry of Economy

      Export pipelines under way

      The Vaca Muerta Oil Sur pipeline, a USD 2.5 billion RIGI project, will run 437 km from Allen to an export terminal at Punta Colorada on the Atlantic coast.
